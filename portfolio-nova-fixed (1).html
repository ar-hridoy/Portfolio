01 · About

Curious about data, serious about impact.

A quick snapshot of who I am and where I'm headed.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at Daffodil International University with a CGPA of 3.99 (up to 5th semester), focused on data science and machine learning.

Why data science? Because every dataset holds a decision waiting to be made. I enjoy the full path — cleaning messy data, finding the story inside it, building a model, and turning the result into something a person can act on.

Outside coursework I'm Co-Founder & CFO of StratifyX, where I get to apply analytics to real financial decisions. I'm also Prefect of the Programming & Problem Solving Lab, mentoring students, and a problem setter for the DIU Competitive Programming Club.

My mission: build intelligent systems that transform data into impactful decisions — and keep sharing what I learn along the way.
